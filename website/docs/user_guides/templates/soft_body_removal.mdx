Removing a soft-body (<rapier>`PhysicsWorld::remove_soft_body`</rapier><bevy>i.e., despawning its entity, or removing
its `SoftBody` component</bevy><js>`World.removeSoftBody`</js><c>`r3RemoveSoftBody`</c><py>`PhysicsWorld.remove_soft_body`</py>) removes everything the engine created for it: its root
body, the proxies of its clusters, the colliders of its surface and of its deformable meshes, as well as the joints
attached to any of them. One cluster can also be removed on its own
(<rapier>`PhysicsWorld::remove_soft_body_cluster`</rapier><bevy>by despawning its entity, or removing its
`SoftBodyCluster` component</bevy><js>`World.removeSoftBodyCluster`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_RemoveCluster`</c><py>`PhysicsWorld.remove_soft_body_cluster`</py>):

:::warning
Removing a cluster also removes the particles that only this cluster covered, with their elements and their
attachments. Therefore removing the last cluster of a soft-body removes the soft-body itself. Note that removing the
proxy of a cluster <notc>from the rigid-body set</notc><c>with `r3RemoveRigidBody`</c><py> (with `PhysicsWorld.remove_body`, or with `RigidBodySet.remove` given the soft-body set as its `soft_bodies` argument, without which it raises a `ValueError`)</py> is equivalent to removing the cluster.
:::
