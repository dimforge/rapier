Forces and impulses can be applied to a soft-body as a whole
(<rapier>`add_force`, `apply_impulse`</rapier><bevy>`SoftBodyExternalForce::force`,
`SoftBodyExternalImpulse::velocity_change`</bevy><js>`addForce`, `applyImpulse`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_AddForce`, `r3SoftBody_ApplyImpulse`</c><py>`add_force`, `apply_impulse`</py>),
or to one particular particle
(<rapier>`add_particle_force`, `apply_particle_impulse`</rapier><bevy>`SoftBodyExternalForce::particle_forces`,
`SoftBodyExternalImpulse::particle_impulses`</bevy><js>`addParticleForce`, `applyParticleImpulse`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_AddParticleForce`, `r3SoftBody_ApplyParticleImpulse`</c><py>`add_particle_force`, `apply_particle_impulse`</py>).
A force added to the whole body is added to each of its free particles and is persistent, i.e., it keeps being applied
at each step until the forces are reset (<rapier>`reset_forces`</rapier><bevy>i.e., until the `SoftBodyExternalForce` component
changes or is removed</bevy><js>`resetForces`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_ResetForces`</c><py>`reset_forces`</py>), exactly like the forces of
a [rigid-body](./rigid_bodies.mdx#forces-and-impulses). An impulse applied to the whole body is a velocity change
applied to each of its free particles, so the body is kicked as a whole without being deformed. Note that the pinned
particles ignore both.

Two additional methods are provided for the effects which magnitude depends on the distance to a point: an impulse
applied within a radius
(<notjs>`apply_impulse_at_point`</notjs><js>`applyImpulseAtPoint`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_ApplyImpulseAtPoint`</c><py>`apply_impulse_at_point`</py>), and a
radial blast pushing the particles away from its center
(<notjs>`apply_radial_impulse`</notjs><js>`applyRadialImpulse`</js><c>`r3SoftBody_ApplyRadialImpulse`</c><py>`apply_radial_impulse`</py>). In both
cases the impulse is scaled linearly down to zero at the given radius. Like for the rigid-bodies,
<notpy>the last boolean argument</notpy><py>the `wake_up` argument (`True` by default)</py> of all these methods ensures the soft-body is
[awake](./rigid_bodies.mdx#sleeping) before the force or the impulse is applied:
