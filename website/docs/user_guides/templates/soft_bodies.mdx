_For a high-level overview of the methods behind our soft-body implementation, see our [blog-post](https://dimforge.com/blog/2026/09/25/soft-bodies-in-the-rapier-physics-engine)._

Rigid-bodies can't deform in any way, which is precisely what makes them cheap and easy to control. Soft-bodies, aka.
_deformable bodies_, are made for everything that must bend, stretch, or squash: ropes, cloth, jelly, balloons, the
chassis of a car denting on impact, etc. They live in the same world as the [rigid-bodies](./rigid_bodies.mdx) and the
[colliders](./colliders.mdx), and interact with all the other features of the engine with little to no restriction:
- Any kind of [impulse joint](./joints.mdx) can be attached to a soft-body through its
  [soft frames](./soft_bodies.mdx#soft-frames-joints-and-rigid-colliders), which allows the definition of links between
  two soft-bodies, between a soft-body and a rigid-body, or between a soft-body and a
  [multibody](./joint_constraints.mdx#multibodies).
- [Deformable colliders](./soft_bodies.mdx#deformable-colliders), as well as [rigid ones](./soft_bodies.mdx#clusters),
  can be attached to a soft-body, sensors included.
- A detailed mesh can follow the deformations of a coarser simulated lattice
  ([**skinning**](./soft_bodies.mdx#skinned-soft-bodies)).
- Parts of a soft-body can be [controlled kinematically](./soft_bodies.mdx#particles-and-kinematic-control).
- A soft-body can deform permanently ([**plasticity**](./soft_bodies.mdx#plasticity)), and tear apart
  ([**tearing**](./soft_bodies.mdx#tearing)).

<bevy>

:::info
In Bevy, a soft-body is an entity with a `SoftBody` component, and most of its properties are
controlled by components too. The conventions linking the entity to the soft-body (its transform, the rigid-bodies and
the colliders it stands for, etc.) are detailed in the
[soft-bodies and entities](./soft_bodies.mdx#soft-bodies-and-entities) section. Note that the
[FEM solver](./soft_bodies.mdx#fem-solver) requires the `fem` feature of `bevy_rapier`.
:::

</bevy>
<c>

:::info
The [FEM solver](./soft_bodies.mdx#fem-solver) requires the library to be built with the `fem` feature (see
[building the C bindings](./getting_started_c.mdx#building-the-c-bindings)).
:::

</c>
<py>

:::info
The Python bindings are 3D only: whenever the following sections mention 2D bodies (their triangle cells, the area they
enclose, or the constructors existing only in 2D), only the 3D counterpart applies. Note that the particles and the
elements of a soft-body are read as [NumPy](https://numpy.org) arrays, and can be given either as NumPy arrays or as
sequences of tuples.
:::

</py>
