**Rapier** is developed and maintained by [Dimforge](https://dimforge.com),
and welcomes contributions from the community. In this page you will see various ways of getting
help and contributing.

## Let's talk
You are encouraged to join us on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/vt9DJSW)! We are
always happy to help, and sometimes a single question there could save you loads of time
skimming through the documentation.

## Working on the library or the website

If you intend to work on the source code of **Rapier** (or its official bindings or website),
you should start by [forking](https://help.github.com/articles/fork-a-repo) the
[repository](https://github.com/dimforge/rapier). Once you are done making
modifications to your own copy of **Rapier**, you have to [create a pull
request](https://help.github.com/articles/creating-a-pull-request) targeting
the **master** branch so that your contribution can be reviewed, commented, and
eventually merged.
